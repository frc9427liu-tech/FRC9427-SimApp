# FRC9427 Simulator — Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated: 2026-10-11

## English
FRC9427 Simulator is a free desktop FRC robot simulator made by a student team (FRC 9427).

- **Google sign-in (optional).** If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app requests only your basic profile (name, email address) through Google's standard OAuth flow. This is used only to show your name in the in-game profile.
- **Where data goes.** Your name, email, avatar picture, level, rank and match history are stored **only on your own computer**. The app does not upload them to any server and we do not receive them. The Google access token is not saved.
- **No tracking, no ads, no sale or sharing of data.**
- **Guest mode** works without any Google account.
- **Deleting data.** Delete the game's save folder (`%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\FRC9427\FRC9427 Simulator`) or use "Switch/Reset player" to remove everything.
- **Update check.** The app may contact GitHub to check for new versions; no personal data is sent.

Contact: frc9427liu@gmail.com

FRC9427 模擬器是由學生隊伍(FRC 9427)製作的免費桌面 FRC 機器人模擬器。

- **訪客模式**不需要 Google 帳號。
- **刪除資料**:刪除遊戲存檔資料夾(`%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\FRC9427\FRC9427 Simulator`)即可。
